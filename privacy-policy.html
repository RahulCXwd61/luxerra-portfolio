Privacy Policy

Last Update: 21 June, 2025

At Luxerra, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the data you provide when using our website and booking services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

• Personal details: Name, phone number, and email address

• Booking details: Selected car, pick-up and drop-off dates

• Optional information: Special requests or comments

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used for:

• Processing and confirming your bookings

• Responding to inquiries or requests

• Improving our services and user experience

• Sending promotional emails, only if you subscribe to our newsletter

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal data. Your information may be shared with:

• Service providers necessary to complete your booking (e.g., payment processors)

• Authorities when required by law

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

• Access and update your personal information

• Request deletion of your data

• Opt-out of marketing communications

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance user experience. You can manage cookie settings in your browser.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on our website.
